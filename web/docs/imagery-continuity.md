# Stable imagery during navigation — 2026-11-03

The movement optimization introduced on November 2 raised the globe's screen-space error from 1.2 to 2.4 on every moveStart and restored it 150 ms after moveEnd. This changes the selected terrain quadtree and its attached imagery, not just background loading priority. Previously loaded detail could visibly drop during zoom/pan. Remove this motion-dependent downgrade; keep the existing settled tolerance, bounded 256-tile globe cache and ancestor-preload settings throughout movement. Initial local overview bootstrap and coarse-terrain acceleration remain unchanged.

Two imagery-provider continuity problems are also corrected without increasing memory limits:

- The 64-image decoded cache was FIFO even on a hit. Reusing an image or fallback ancestor now refreshes its recency, so nearby arrivals do not evict a repeatedly used image prematurely.
- Cancellation previously passed through the generic failure fallback and could be treated as a successful coarse tile. Cancelled consumers now reject normally. A failed duplicate checks for a successful same-address result both before and after asynchronous fallback work; late coarse results cannot replace that completed image.

Keep Cesium's normal ready-ancestor/child refinement, genuine missing-data masks, bounded damaged-transfer fallback, source level metadata and request throttling. There is no screen-capture overlay, synthetic sharpening, unbounded tile retention, or fabricated source detail. Optical/native data, routes and scientific calculations are unchanged. Unvisited areas still need data; genuine source resolution remains the limit. Holding the settled detail tolerance during movement can require more rendering than the removed coarsening optimization; no FPS benchmark was performed.

Engine behavior reference: https://cesium.com/learn/cesiumjs/ref-doc/Globe.html and the pinned 1.145 QuadtreePrimitive source at https://github.com/CesiumGS/cesium/blob/1.145/packages/engine/Source/Scene/QuadtreePrimitive.js . No private Cesium APIs are patched.
