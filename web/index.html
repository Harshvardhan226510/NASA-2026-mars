    Sources & display accuracy

    Positions use NASA/JPL MAR099 ephemerides and SPICE IAU rotation models. These are geometric modeled positions, not live observations. Coverage: 2025–2035. Moon paths show approximately one revolution. Spacecraft paths follow actual JPL vectors, clipped to one angular revolution around the displayed time; incomplete coverage remains an open arc. Brighter trails show past travel. Positions are interpolated at the current UTC without extrapolation. Pause freezes a dated snapshot; Return to now catches up to current UTC. JPL spacecraft trajectories can include planning predictions. Missing coverage is left unknown. MAVEN is hidden after 4 December 2025 because JPL flags its predicted orbit as unreliable.

    NASA/JPL visualization meshes supplied by you. Texture and prime-meridian alignment are approximate. The moons are 3D objects; Mars imagery and route tools apply to Mars’s surface. The camera-altitude meter continues to refer to Mars.

        Model & accuracy

        Original partner engine: energy-aware 8-neighbour A*, Minetti walking-cost curve, suit-mass and gravity factors, artificial potential field attraction, and exact permutations for the checkpoint order. The ordering uses straight grid distances; its legs use terrain costs.

        Energy and oxygen are model estimates, not a validated EVA budget. Routes check sampled elevation and slopes; they do not yet assess rocks, soil strength, weather, radiation or life support. Heuristic search and smoothing do not guarantee a globally optimal or astronaut-safe path.

        Grid alignment uses metre coordinates. Broad-area grids have increasing projection distortion and coarser sampling; refine sections for local decisions. Smoothing checks the entire sampled corridor. A or B may be moved to a walkable grid cell; adjustments appear with the result. Display relief exaggeration never changes routing heights.
