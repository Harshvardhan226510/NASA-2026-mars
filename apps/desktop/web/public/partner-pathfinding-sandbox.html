Mission Commander FAQ
Q1: Why not a straight ruler line?
Mars is not flat! The straight line between Start & Goal cuts directly through a 45° vertical cliff wall (click the red button above to test). Walking off a 45° cliff is 100% fatal. Paths curve through the gentle 13° canyon pass to keep the astronaut alive.
Q2: What does Astronaut Mass do?
A light astronaut (80 kg) easily hikes over the 15° hill at (50, 22) to save distance. A heavy astronaut (160 kg) carrying heavy gear suffers exponential muscle exhaustion on slopes, forcing the pathfinder to divert around the hill into flatter valleys.
Q3: How do R1, R2 & R3 differ?
• R1 Safe: Emergency exit. Ignores minerals completely.
• R2 APF: Remote-sensing flyby. Curves into mineral sensor range without stopping.
• R3 Deep: Ground drilling expedition. Intercepts every deposit directly in optimal order.
Q4: Is anything hardcoded?
Zero hardcoding! Click & drag Start, Goal, or any Mineral with your mouse, or slide Mass/APF, and watch A* recompute all routes live across 6,400 terrain cells!
